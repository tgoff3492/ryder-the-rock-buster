# Weston Super Speeder 🏎️💨

A top-down racing game for **Weston**. Pick a ride, pick a track, and race 4 computer cars, or race a grown-up on the same keyboard.

## ▶ Play it now

**https://tgoff3492.github.io/ryder-the-rock-buster/super-speeder/** (or pick it from the [Goff Kid's Arcade](../README.md) menu)

## Two ways to drive

Click the mode button on the title screen to switch. Each player picks their own mode.

| | 🐣 Little Kid Mode | 🏁 Big Kid Mode |
|---|---|---|
| Go | Hold **any arrow key** | **↑** gas, **↓** brake |
| Steer | The car stays on the road by itself. **← →** slides across the road to grab stars and prizes | **← →** steer for real. Driving on the grass slows you down |
| Prizes | Go off by themselves after a second (or press **SPACE**) | Press **SPACE** |
| Honk | **↓** | **Enter** |
| Computer cars | Stay just behind you, so you win | Actually try to win |
| Laps | 2 | 3 |

There's no crashing and no losing: every race ends with a trophy and confetti.

## Keys

| | Player 1 | Player 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Drive | Arrow keys | W A S D |
| Use a prize / nitro | SPACE | Q |
| Honk (Big Kid) | Enter | E |

**P** = pause, **M** = sound on/off, **Esc** = back.
With one player, either side of the keyboard works.

## Stuff on the track

- **Boost pads** (blue arrows): drive over them to ZOOM
- **⭐ Stars**: fill up your **NITRO** bar. When it's full, press SPACE for a blue-flame boost
- **? Prize boxes** give you one of these:

| Prize | What it does |
|---|---|
| 🚀 Turbo Rocket | Big burst of speed |
| 🟢 Slime Splat | Drops slime behind you; cars that hit it spin out |
| 🫧 Bubble Shield | Blocks the next thing that would spin you out |
| 🌟 Super Star | You get bigger, faster, rainbow-colored, and bump other cars into spins |
| 📣 Mega Honk | A giant HONK that spins out cars near you |

Computer cars never use slime, honks, or stars against a Little Kid player.

## Unlocks (saved on the computer)

- **Tracks:** finish a race to open the next one: Sunny Meadow → Sandy Beach → Snowy Mountain → Candy Land → Moon Base
- **Stickers:** stars you collect add up. Racing Stripes are free; then Flames (10 ⭐), Lightning (25), Polka Dots (40), Stars (60), Rainbow (90)
